04 · why vefa

Why Vefa Global?

Since 2016, we have been turning business opportunities that reach from India to the world into tangible results.

As Vefa Global Consultancy Pvt. Ltd., based in New Delhi, we provide companies with end-to-end business support in:

  • International trade
  • Import and export
  • Business development
  • Market research
  • IT solutions

By combining our business connections across markets, India and Turkey above all, with local market knowledge and field experience, we help businesses enter new markets, reach the right partners and grow their commercial opportunities.

For us, consulting is not limited to giving advice. From supplier research to identifying partners, from commercial negotiations to operational coordination, from documentation to building digital processes, we take an active role at every stage the project needs.

What sets us apart is that we don't just tell you what needs to be done; we also take responsibility for getting it done.

Knowing that every business has different goals, resources and needs, we develop tailored approaches instead of standard solutions. Our aim is not only to create new opportunities, but to help them grow into sustainable business relationships and long-term commercial value.

Vefa Global: local expertise, international connections and a hands-on business partnership.
