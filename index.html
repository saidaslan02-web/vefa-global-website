About Vefa Global

A consulting partner built for global business.

Founded in 2016 in New Delhi, Vefa Global Private Limited was created to help ambitious businesses navigate complex, fast-moving markets. What began as a single consulting practice has grown into three specialist divisions, each built around deep, hands-on expertise rather than generalist advice.

We work as an extension of your team — sourcing the right materials, building your digital presence, and managing the marketplace accounts that drive your online sales.

  • Specialized expertise across import/export, social media, and e-commerce
  • Hands-on support from strategy through day-to-day execution
  • A network of suppliers and partners spanning multiple markets
  • Direct, senior-level attention on every engagement

01 · Import / Export Consulting

Sourcing and moving the right product, across categories.

Import and export is a business of relationships, quality control, and logistics — whatever is moving through the supply chain. We guide manufacturers, traders, and retailers through every step, from identifying reliable suppliers and verifying product quality, to navigating shipping, customs, and documentation.

Marble Trolley Bags Basmati Rice
  • Supplier identification & quality verification
  • Price negotiation and contract support
  • Shipping, customs & import/export documentation
  • Quality control and on-site inspection support

Sourcing & export regions: Italy, Turkey, the Middle East & India

02 · Social Media Management

Building brands that people actually follow.

A strong social presence takes more than posting consistently — it takes strategy. We help brands define their voice, plan content that performs, and manage day-to-day activity across platforms.

  • Content strategy & editorial calendars
  • Day-to-day platform management
  • Paid campaign planning & performance tracking
  • Community management & audience growth

Platforms: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn & TikTok

03 · E-Commerce Consulting

Marketplace accounts that sell consistently.

Selling on a marketplace is easy — selling profitably and consistently is not. We manage and optimize seller accounts on Amazon, Flipkart, and Etsy, handling everything from listings to performance.

  • Account setup, health & compliance management
  • Listing optimization, pricing & catalog strategy
  • Advertising & promotions to drive sales
  • Order, review & performance management

Marketplaces: Amazon, Flipkart & Etsy
